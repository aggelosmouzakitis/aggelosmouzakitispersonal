Somo Flow · Movement experience Coming soon

Somo Flow

A movement class that combines accessible yoga with somatic shaking, with the focus on the body rather than conversation.

Join the waiting list→See what happens in a class →

What it is

Movement first, with awareness

In Somo Flow we use yoga and gentle movement to prepare the body, and then, or alongside it, we add somatic shaking.

The purpose is to notice physical tension, move with more awareness and experience a different relationship with your body.

It is mainly a body-based class. It has less conversation than Somo Circles and does not ask you to share personal experiences.

Difference

Somo Flow and Somo Circles

The two experiences share somatic shaking, but they have a different purpose.

Somo Flow

  • Led by movement.
  • Includes little or no personal sharing.
  • Is not group therapy.
  • Suits people who want a body-focused experience.

Somo Circles

  • Combines somatic shaking with group therapy.
  • Includes facilitated reflection.
  • Allows deeper exploration of personal experience.

Neither one is higher or more advanced. They are simply different.

A class

What happens in a Somo Flow

01
Arrival and grounding
A quiet start to land in the space and in your body.
02
Accessible yoga and movement
Gentle movements suited to beginners, with adaptations where needed.
03
Somatic shaking
Positions that let the shaking appear, at your own pace.
04
Rest and awareness
Time to notice how the body feels after moving.
05
Closing
A calm return to the usual rhythm of the day.

Who it may suit

It may interest you if you are looking for

  • More awareness of the body.
  • Accessible movement, friendly to beginners.
  • An alternative to a conventional yoga class.
  • A way to try somatic shaking without joining a therapy group.
  • A structured pause from a mentally demanding routine.

How to explain it to someone else

Somo Flow is a movement class that combines accessible yoga with somatic shaking. It is designed for people who want to move, notice physical tension and become more aware of their body, without joining a therapy group.
