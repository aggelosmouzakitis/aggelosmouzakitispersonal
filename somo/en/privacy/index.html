Who we are

SOMO creates small group experiences. For questions about your data, contact us using the details at the end of this page.

Contact forms

When you complete a contact form, we receive the details you give us so we can reply. We do not use them for anything unrelated to your request.

Somo Outdoors applications

The Somo Outdoors application includes basic professional details, so we can understand whether the community is a fit. We do not ask for sensitive data.

Waiting lists and updates

If you join a waiting list, we use your details only to keep you informed about that particular experience.

Bookings through Luma

Bookings and waiting lists are handled through the Luma platform. Luma is an external provider with its own privacy policy, which we suggest you read.

Analytics and cookies

The site may use basic analytics to understand how it is used. If cookies are enabled, there will be a notice about them.

Your rights

You can request access to your data or its deletion. Contact us and we will respond.

Contact

For anything related to privacy, contact us through the form or the email shown on the site.
