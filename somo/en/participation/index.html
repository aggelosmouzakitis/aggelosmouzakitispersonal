Participation Conditions

What applies when you book and take part in a SOMO experience.

Bookings

Bookings are made through Luma. Each event has its own link, its own places and its own fee.

Payments

Where a payment applies, it is made through Luma, according to what is stated on the event.

Cancellations

Cancellation terms are shown on each event on Luma. Review them before you book.

Personal responsibility

You take part at your own responsibility and decide whether an activity is suitable for you. See the Safety and FAQ page as well.

Respect for the group

We ask for respect towards other participants and the facilitator. Hard pitching or disruptive behaviour is not acceptable.

Confidentiality in therapeutic groups

In therapeutic groups, what participants share stays within the group.

The facilitator's role

The facilitator may stop someone's participation when this is necessary for the safety or wellbeing of the group.

Outdoor events

Outdoor events may change or be postponed for weather or safety reasons.

Photography

Photographs are taken only with the clear consent of participants.

No guaranteed outcome

We do not promise specific therapeutic or wellbeing outcomes.
