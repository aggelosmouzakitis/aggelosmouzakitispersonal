Safety and FAQ

Safety and participation

Practical information so you can take part with awareness. This page is not medical advice.

General participation principles

Before and during

  • You decide whether an activity is suitable for you.
  • Every physical exercise can be reduced, adapted or stopped.
  • Personal sharing is optional, unless a specific therapeutic group has a different agreement.
  • Discomfort is not proof that an exercise is working.
  • Tell the facilitator when you need support or a pause.
  • Outdoor events have requirements that depend on the activity.
  • SOMO does not replace medical care or individual mental health support.

Health concerns

If you have a relevant health condition, injury, pregnancy, recent surgery or any uncertainty about taking part physically, consult an appropriate health professional first.

Emotional safety

Body-based practices can sometimes bring up emotions, memories or unexpected reactions.

You can pause, stop, rest or choose not to talk about what came up. The facilitator explains the structure and your options before the practice begins.

Notes for each experience

Somo Circles

Somo Circles includes facilitated group therapy, but it does not offer the same level of individual assessment and ongoing support as individual psychotherapy.

If you are in acute crisis or need immediate individual support, please turn to an appropriate individual service.

Somo Flow

Somo Flow is a movement-based group class and not group psychotherapy.

Somo Outdoors

For every outdoor event, review the route difficulty, the expected duration, the weather, clothing, water, transport and the safety notes in advance.

Each event on Luma contains the final practical details.

FAQ

General questions

What is somatic shaking?

It is a natural trembling that can appear when the body tires or relaxes after a simple exercise. You stay alert and in control.

Is shaking compulsory?

No. You set the intensity and you can stop whenever you want.

Do I remain in control?

Yes. You can adapt, pause, rest or leave an exercise at any time.

Do I need to be physically fit?

No. The exercises are adaptable. If you have a health concern, consult a health professional first.

Are SOMO sessions psychotherapy?

Somo Circles includes facilitated group therapy. Somo Flow and Somo Outdoors are not therapy.

Can SOMO replace individual therapy?

No. SOMO does not replace individual psychotherapy or medical care.

What language are sessions delivered in?

Sessions run in Greek or English. The language of each event is shown on Luma.

Are sessions available in English and Greek?

Yes. Some events run in English. Check the language on each event on Luma.

Where do events take place?

In person. The venue and area are listed on each event on Luma.

How do I register?

Registration is through Luma. Each event has its own link.

How do I cancel?

Each event on Luma shows its own booking and cancellation terms.

Can organisations host a private session?

Yes. See the For Organisations page and get in touch.
