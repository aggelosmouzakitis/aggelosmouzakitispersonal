What SOMO is

Three experiences, different levels of participation

SOMO is not one thing. It is three different experiences, with different depth and different purpose. Some are therapeutic. Others focus on movement or on community.

What connects them is simple. All three help you get closer to what is happening in your body and to your relationship with the people around you.

  • Somo Circles combines somatic shaking with group therapy.
  • Somo Flow combines yoga with somatic shaking.
  • Somo Outdoors combines outdoor activity with mindful attention and real interaction.

How it works

Four principles in every SOMO experience

Whichever experience you choose, the work happens the same way.

01
Clear facilitation
Every experience has a facilitator who explains the structure, gives clear instructions and holds the frame from start to finish.
02
Participant choice
You take part at your own pace. You can adapt an exercise, lower the intensity, pause or stop.
03
Body awareness
We start from what is happening in the body, not only from thinking or talking.
04
Real group interaction
The experiences happen in small groups, where the presence of other people matters.

For professionals who refer

Easy to explain to your own people

If you work with people as psychotherapists, counsellors, coaches or yoga and Pilates teachers, SOMO can suit those who want to work with their experience through both the body and a group.

SOMO is not a medical service. Referring someone to an experience is not a medical recommendation, it is simply a suggestion for something that may be useful.

A small community of professionals, outdoors

Somo Outdoors brings together founders, solopreneurs, executives and independent professionals for small outdoor events with real interaction.

Who is behind SOMO
