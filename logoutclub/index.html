Why

Your day has enough screen time.

Work happens there. So do messages, entertainment and most of the plans we never end up making.

Logout Club gives you an easy reason to put the phone away and do something that is actually happening around you.

There is no digital detox lecture and nothing you need to improve about yourself. You simply show up, put the screen away and take part.

Experiences

Different ways to log out.

01

Slow Sundays

A few unhurried hours in one place. Read, write, draw, try some Journaling, solve Puzzles or bring any offline activity you enjoy. We begin with protected quiet time and leave room for conversation, games and shared activities afterwards, only if you feel like joining.

Editions may include

Offline Hours · Reading Parties · Writing Hours · Draw & Make · Journaling · Puzzles · Bring Your Own Offline Thing

02

Offline Walks

Easy-going walks through the city or out in nature, without spending the entire route checking a screen. Some walks are simply about walking. Others may include photography, sketching, observation prompts or a place worth stopping for.

Editions may include

Nature Walks · City Walks · Photo Walks · Sunset Walks · Easy Hikes · Walk & Sketch

03

Game Nights

Card games, board games and original formats that make it easy to participate without forced introductions or awkward small talk. Terrible Advice Club is one of the original games hosted by Logout Club. More games and questionable decisions will follow.

Editions may include

Terrible Advice Club · Analog Games · Card Games · Board Games · Team Challenges

04

Special Editions

Occasional experiments that do not fit neatly into the regular programme. A phone-free picnic, collaborative cooking, a museum visit, live music, a day trip or something suggested by the community. We will try the ideas that receive the most interest.

Possible editions

Phone-free Picnics · Collaborative Cooking · Museum Visits · Supper Clubs · Day Trips · Community Experiments

The activity comes first.

Logout Club is not a networking event and you will not be asked to pitch yourself to a room of strangers.

Every event gives people something worth doing together. That makes conversation easier, but conversation is never compulsory.

Come alone or bring someone. Either works.

How it works

Four steps. That’s the whole thing.

  1. 1

    Choose an event

    Pick something you would actually enjoy doing, even if you did not meet anyone new.

  2. 2

    Show up

    Come alone or bring someone. You do not need to prepare anything unless the event description asks you to bring a book, game or other material.

  3. 3

    Let your phone rest

    Phones stay switched off and out of sight. The exact setup may change depending on the event.

  4. 4

    Take part

    Each event has a host and a simple structure. Enough guidance to remove the awkwardness, without turning your free time into another workshop.

Who it’s for

Built for people who live online.

Developers Founders Designers Creators Remote workers

Developers, founders, designers, creators, remote workers and anyone else whose work, entertainment and social life happen largely through a screen.

You do not need to work in tech. You only need to want a few hours away from your phone.

Closing the laptop is not always the same as logging out.

We finish working and open another screen. Rest becomes scrolling, conversation becomes messaging and an empty evening disappears without us quite noticing.

Logout Club is not a productivity hack. It simply puts something genuinely offline in your calendar before another screen fills the space.
