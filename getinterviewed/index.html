Get interviewed

I'm interviewing founders and solopreneurs in tech about what it actually took to get where they are.

My conversations focus on the patterns you had to fight, what it cost you and for how long, who or what helped, and what it's like to live under that kind of pressure day to day. The comparisons you make in your head. What keeps you going when it would be easier to stop. The stories you don't usually tell.

I do these because this is the stuff people feel constantly and almost never say out loud. Hearing someone else say it plainly helps more than any advice.

If you've built something and you're up for an honest conversation about the cost of it, not just the wins, I'd like to talk to you.
