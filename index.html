01 / BUSINESS

I did the work before I started selling advice.

Eighteen years in product and growth. Seven of them running my own consultancy and working with 100+ tech companies. I also built two startups. Neither went well.

I know business because I made the decisions and lived with the consequences.

02 / CAREER

I built my own career before advising anyone on theirs.

I built it abroad, through roles in product and growth, and I still work at IBM.

I did not leave a job, rebrand as a coach and start talking about a career I never had.

03 / TRAINING

I am not just a guy with too much confidence.

My training includes an MSc in Integrative Counselling & Psychotherapy from the University of Derby, a Graduate Certificate in Psychology, supervised practice and five years of experience doing this work.

I do not improvise the psychological part.

04 / REACH

I am not a social media personality or an influencer.

A large audience proves you have found a way to earn attention.

Being good at the work and being good at attracting attention are two completely different skills.

If you want a performer, there are better choices.
