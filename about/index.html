About

Aggelos Mouzakitis

High-Performance Coach & Licensed Therapist
BACP-registered · Based in Ireland · Working globally

I help tech high performers find genuine fulfilment without losing their ambition — breaking the deeper patterns behind burnout, shame-driven achievement, overthinking, and emotional fatigue, through high-trust advisory, nervous system regulation, and data.

Background

Before training as a therapist, I spent 18+ years in B2B SaaS growth, from early-stage startups through to IBM's enterprise portfolio, advising 50+ companies. I still work as a fractional growth advisor. That history means I understand the environment my clients operate in — founder stress, leadership pressure, career decisions, difficult conversations — without needing half a session to set the context.

How I work

High-trust advisory. Grounded in your real professional context rather than abstract coaching.

Nervous system regulation. The work includes the body, not just thoughts: how pressure shows up as activation, shutdown, and poor recovery, alongside shame, identity, and the meanings you attach to success and failure.

Data. Wearable and behavioural signals — sleep, HRV, stress, recovery — make invisible stress patterns visible. Not biohacking. Pattern recognition.

Who I work with

Founders, executives, product and growth leaders, and senior operators in tech — people doing well by every external measure who sense their relationship with work, performance, and self-worth has become too costly.

Credentials

Licensed psychotherapist and registered member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP). All sessions are online, one-on-one, and confidential.

Writing

I write about the psychology of ambition, identity, and performance at Undisguised, and publish essays in my writing. The writing names what most people in high-stakes careers feel but rarely say out loud. The private work is where we change it.
