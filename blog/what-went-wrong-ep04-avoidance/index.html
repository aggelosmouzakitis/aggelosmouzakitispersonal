What Went Wrong — Ep. 04: Avoidance

Why the smartest founders are exceptional at avoiding the work that matters.

Jul 5, 2026 · Aggelos Mouzakitis

Some of the most productive founders you'll meet are also the most avoidant — they avoid brilliantly. This episode of What Went Wrong looks at the version of avoidance that doesn't look like avoidance at all: 14-hour days spent perfecting positioning instead of doing sales, endless research that stands in for action, polished psychological explanations that keep the real work at arm's length.

If you've ever felt stuck, worked hard without moving forward, or quietly suspected you're afraid of the very success you say you want, this conversation is aimed squarely at you.

Aggelos and co-host Manuel Mendez take avoidance apart from both the coach's chair and their own lived experience as operators. What sets this apart from the usual "just be disciplined" take is that it refuses the easy answer. Aggelos draws on years of working with founders — and on his own patterns — to show why powering through often backfires and where the line between doing the hard thing and harming yourself truly sits.

Tune in to hear about:

  • The type of avoidance that masks as productivity — and why being "badass" at the wrong task is the most convincing disguise of all
  • Why so many ambitious people are secretly protecting themselves from success, and what that has to do with responsibility
  • What Aggelos hears when a client asks him to "help with accountability" — and the clarifying question he asks back before agreeing
  • How hiring a coach can itself become a sophisticated form of hiding
  • Where the "just do it, power through" advice is genuinely appropriate — and the specific moment it tips over into being harmful
  • How things you avoided as a child may have quietly built the identity, career and company you have today
