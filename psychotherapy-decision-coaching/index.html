Common questions

What can I bring to the session?

A career decision, business dilemma, cofounder or client conflict, difficult conversation, loss of motivation, anxiety, imposter feelings or a general sense that something is no longer right. Bring the issue that is taking up the most space now.

What if I cannot explain what the problem is?

You do not need a diagnosis or a perfectly framed question. “Something feels wrong and I cannot work out what” is enough to begin.

Is this only for founders?

No. It is for founders, freelancers and experienced professionals dealing with problems where work, ambition and personal psychology overlap.

Is this therapy or coaching?

Neither cleanly. I am more direct and action-oriented than a conventional therapist, and I do not use a coaching framework for every problem. We work at the level the situation actually requires.

Will you tell me what to do?

I will tell you what I think and challenge the parts of your reasoning that do not hold up. I will not make the decision for you.

Can we work on anxiety, imposter syndrome or lost motivation?

Yes. We do not stop at managing the symptom or trying to think more positively. We look at what keeps producing it and what needs to change.

Can I bring something personal?

Yes. Relationships, identity, health and purpose do not remain separate from work simply because your calendar says they should. We follow the problem without losing sight of why you came.

Is this confidential?

The work is private and governed by professional confidentiality. Any limits to confidentiality are explained clearly before we begin.

Is the Sparring Session just a sales call?

No. It has a standalone outcome. You should leave with a clearer understanding of what is happening and what to do next, even if we never work together.

What happens afterwards?

You can take the outcome away and act on it yourself. If deeper or ongoing work would be useful, we can discuss that after the session.
